## Project team

The following roles form the core delivery team. Named resources are confirmed at kickoff; roles and responsibilities remain consistent throughout delivery.

- **Project Manager** — owns the project plan, schedule, scope control and delivery governance; facilitates kickoff, weekly status and sprint reviews; manages risks, issues, dependencies and stakeholder communications.
- **Solution Engineer** — defines the end-to-end solution architecture, data model and integration approach; translates business requirements into workflow patterns, the security model and the technical design; ensures scalable, auditable, compliance-aligned design decisions.
- **Implementation Consultant (Configuration Lead)** — configures workflows, forms, business rules, roles/permissions and notifications; builds routing, task queues, approval steps and lifecycle states; supports user workshops, prototyping and iterative refinement. The Solution Engineer and Implementation Lead roles may be fulfilled by the same resource, depending on the final scope of work.
- **QA / Testing Lead** — defines the test strategy, acceptance criteria and traceability from requirements to test cases; coordinates test execution and defect triage across iterations and UAT.
- **Support / Customer Success (post-go-live)** — leads hypercare, triages requests and coordinates fixes; provides onboarding reinforcement, adoption tracking and operational best practices; ensures an ongoing improvement cadence and knowledge transfer.

## Implementation methodology

Our implementation approach is designed for low delivery risk and rapid time to value:

- **Agile, iterative delivery** — working increments in short cycles so stakeholders validate early and rework is minimised.
- **Configuration first** — platform configuration is prioritised over custom development to accelerate delivery and reduce technical risk.
- **Regular stakeholder feedback** — frequent demonstrations and structured feedback loops keep the solution aligned and scope changes controlled.

**Phase 1 — Discovery & Design (estimated 2–3 weeks).** Confirm requirements and success criteria; map workflows and lifecycle states; define the data structure, the role and permission model, reporting/audit needs and integration requirements. Deliverables: requirements baseline and prioritised backlog.

**Phase 2 — Configuration (estimated 4–6 weeks).** Configure workflows, forms, roles, permissions, business rules and integration routines; implement routing, approvals, notifications, SLAs and validations. Deliverables: demo-ready solution increments and an updated enhancements backlog.

**Phase 3 — Testing / UAT (estimated 2–3 weeks).** Validate workflows end to end with business users; log, triage and resolve defects; regression-test updated workflows. Deliverables: UAT sign-off against the defined acceptance criteria and a finalised configuration and readiness checklist.

**Phase 4 — Deployment (estimated 1 week).** Production readiness review, go-live cutover, user training and role-based enablement. Deliverables: live production deployment and operational handover.

**Phase 5 — Post-go-live support (hypercare).** Stabilise operations, monitor adoption, capture feedback and deliver minor iterative improvements on a predictable release cadence.

Timelines are refined during Discovery based on scope, stakeholder availability and any integration or migration requirements.

## Security and compliance evidence package

Nutrient's security and compliance documentation is shared under NDA through the Nutrient Trust Center, and access is granted to the evaluation team on request. The evidence package typically includes:

- Independent audit reports and attestations
- Subprocessor list and hosting provider overview
- Encryption in transit and at rest, and key management overview
- Identity and access management: SSO configuration, MFA enforcement options, RBAC / permission model
- Audit logging and monitoring overview (what is logged, access and export options)
- Data residency, backup and disaster recovery statements
- Accessibility conformance documentation
- AI / ML governance, data flows and third-party provider disclosures (where AI features are proposed)

Items the solicitation explicitly requires in the response body are marked for confirmation by the security team before submission.
