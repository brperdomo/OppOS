## Past relevant experience

**GSK standardises global IT request management with Nutrient Workflow** — GlaxoSmithKline, global pharmaceutical company. Diverse, disconnected IT request processes across departments and geographies produced a confusing user experience, redundant effort and the cost of custom-built request systems. Nutrient Workflow was expanded to standardise and centralise IT request management, including directory integration for pre-population and custom functions, with training and support for rollout across teams. Outcomes: adoption scaled to more than 78,000 users across 60+ countries; a single "one-stop shop" request interface; faster fulfilment (one request type improved from 13 to 8 days at the 90th percentile; a 79% reduction in fulfilment time across the worst-performing processes); and a 77% reduction in development time and 96% cost reduction for new request types. Evidence: https://www.nutrient.io/blog/customers-glaxosmithkline/

**BP streamlines financial approvals and compliance with Nutrient Workflow** — BP, global energy company. Paper-based finance memos and non-standardised approval chains caused delays, visibility gaps and SOX compliance risk. Nutrient Workflow standardised finance requests and approvals with audit trails and reporting, integrated with SAP without duplicating core SAP financial functionality. Outcomes: approval cycles reduced from weeks to as little as one day; real-time visibility into hundreds of active projects synced to SAP; an auditable trail with correct routing through delegation of authority. Evidence: https://www.nutrient.io/blog/customers-bp/

Reusable starter templates illustrate typical workflows: https://www.nutrient.io/guides/workflow-automation/workflow-templates/

**How outcomes are measured.** Task completion time studies (before/after), error-rate and rework tracking, funnel analytics (drop-off and step completion), first-time-user success tests, and support ticket volume and knowledge-base usage trends.
